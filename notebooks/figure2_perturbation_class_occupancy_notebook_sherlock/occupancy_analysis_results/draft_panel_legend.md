Figure 2. Perturbation classes redistribute cells among distinct developmental states.
Rows denote reviewed regulator classes and columns denote day-specific author-defined coarse states. For each target and state, adjusted one-versus-rest logistic regression was performed separately by collection day using a target-union carrier indicator and biological sample, pct_counts_mt, log1p(total_counts), log1p(guides_per_cell), log1p(guide_umi_counts), and log1p(n_genes_by_counts) as covariates. Dot color shows the median target-level adjusted log odds ratio within each class. Dot size shows the fraction of evaluable targets with FDR < 0.05 in the direction of the displayed median. Classes with fewer than 4 evaluable targets were omitted. State boundaries were taken from the existing author-defined Leiden mapping and were not re-estimated from target-carrier cells.
