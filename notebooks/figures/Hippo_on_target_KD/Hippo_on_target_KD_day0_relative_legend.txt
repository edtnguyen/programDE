On-target CRISPRi knockdown in hiPSCs (day 0). Open circles denote the control-normalized reference (1), and filled circles denote SCEPTRE-estimated target-gene expression relative to that reference, calculated as 2 raised to the reported log2 fold change. These are effect-based relative-expression estimates, not measured absolute group means. Orange points indicate q ≤ 0.05; black points indicate q > 0.05. Numerical q values: BH adjustment across all 261 QC-passing, finite-p-value tests at day 0 in the supplied CSV. Confidence intervals were unavailable in the supplied data and are not shown.
