# Methods note

The state labels were taken directly from the previous author-defined mapping of `leiden_res_0.5` in `ipsc-ec.Hippo.figure.ipynb`: 0 artEC, 1 iPSC, 2 Neuro-iPSC, 3 PrimStreak, 4 Lat-Meso, 5 Fibro-Mesen, 6 Pre-EC, 7 Neu-Mesen and 8 Others. No target-to-program effects were used and no reclustering was performed in this workflow. The existing UMAP coordinates were used unchanged. However, the original Leiden boundaries were learned from the full CC-Perturb-seq dataset rather than from pure controls alone; this differs from the stricter control-defined-atlas design. Pure controls were identified for descriptive subset plots using: At least one safe-targeting or non-targeting guide and zero real-targeting guides. Cells carrying control and real-targeting guides together are excluded from the pure-control subset.
